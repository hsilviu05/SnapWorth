### Filming notes

- **Real numbers.** A fake total is obvious and it is the one thing that makes
  a reselling audience stop trusting you.
- Shoot the run **before** ending it. Once ended the Activity is gone and you
  cannot re-stage it without scanning again.
- The Dynamic Island only exists on iPhone 14 Pro and later. On anything older
  the run shows on the Lock Screen instead, which is a different — and less
  good — shot.

## Notes

- Both platforms use "link in bio" rather than a pasted URL, because neither
  makes a caption link tappable. The bio link is
  `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snapworth-resale-scanner/id6788521307` — the
  same URL `website/index.html` uses in all four download buttons. The slug
  still reads `snapworth-resale-scanner` from before the rename; Apple routes
  on the numeric ID.
- Post after release, not after approval. Approved is not the same as
  downloadable, and a post whose link 404s is worse than no post.
- The Action Button line only applies to iPhone 15 Pro and later, and Controls
  need iOS 18. Both are stated as conditions rather than as features everyone
  gets, because they aren't.
