# Short-video scripts — YouTube Shorts / TikTok (drafts)

Fifteen 15–30 second scripts in one format: **"Paid X, scanned it, worth Y?"**
Drafts only. Nothing here is posted from the repo.

Accounts: `tiktok.com/@snapworthapp` · YouTube Shorts on the same handle.
Brand: Fraunces + DM Sans · terracotta `#D96C47` · sage `#7A9E7E` ·
cream `#FAF7F4` · dark `#1C1410`.

## Rules every script follows

* **Real finds, real scans, real numbers.** `{PAID}`, `{LOW}`–`{HIGH}`,
  `{SOLD}` and `{FEES}` are filled from an item you actually bought, scanned
  and (where the script says so) sold. Film the scan as it happens. An
  invented price, or a result screen that is not the app's, is a fake
  testimonial, and an obviously fake total reads as a mockup
  (`social_1.4.0.md`). If a script's item never turns up, skip the script.
* **Say what the number is.** On screen it is an *AI estimate* with a
  confidence level, never "sold listings", "comps", "market data" or
  "accurate" (`app_store_listing.md`, *Claims this listing does not make*).
  Where the estimate missed, say so: scripts 8 and 13 are built on that.
* **Free and Pro, said out loud.** One free scan a day. Haul mode, Rack scan,
  Legit check and the listing drafts are Pro; any script that shows one says
  "Pro" on screen.
* **It's your app.** Say so ("I built this app…" or "my app") in the video or
  the caption. TikTok and YouTube both expect it, and it reads better.
* **Values are in US dollars** in the app, whatever the phone's region. Paid
  and sold prices are in the currency you paid and sold in; don't convert.
* Hashtags may name marketplaces. That is fine on social; it is App Store
  keywords where company names are not allowed.

Each script: **Hook** (first line, said and on screen in the first second),
**Shots**, **On-screen text**, **Caption**, **Hashtags**. Timings are targets.

### 1 — The jacket (20 s)
**Hook:** "Paid {PAID} for this jacket. Is it worth anything?"
**Shots:**
1. (0–2 s) Jacket on the rail, hand pulls it out, price tag in frame.
2. (2–6 s) Phone up, SnapWorth camera, shutter.
3. (6–12 s) Result sheet: name, range, confidence band. Hold on the range.
4. (12–20 s) Face to camera: keep or put back.
**On-screen text:** "Paid {PAID}" → "Scanned it…" → "AI estimate: {LOW}–{HIGH}" → "Keeping it?"
**Caption:** Paid {PAID} at the charity shop. One photo, AI resale estimate in seconds. Would you keep it? (My app — one free scan a day.)
**Hashtags:** #thrifting #thriftflip #reseller #vintagejacket #whatsitworth

### 3 — After fees (25 s) · free
**Hook:** "It's worth {HIGH}. But what do I actually make?"
**Shots:**
1. Item in hand, scan, the range (0–6 s).
2. Thrift Flip: type the shop price, pick the marketplace you sell on (6–14 s).
3. The verdict card: profit after {FEES} in fees, ROI (14–22 s).
4. Decision (22–25 s).
**On-screen text:** "Worth {LOW}–{HIGH}?" → "Minus fees…" → "Profit: {PROFIT}" → "Worth flipping?"
**Caption:** The number that matters isn't the price, it's what's left after fees. Thrift Flip is free with the daily scan.
