# App Store Listing — English (U.K.)

English metadata for the UK storefront, written 2026-10-24. The app's English
is the same everywhere; this page changes only the words a UK reseller
searches with and reads: *charity shop* and *car boot*, not *thrift store* and
*yard sale*; *trainers*, not *sneakers*; *preloved*.

Mirrors `app_store_listing.md`, including its accuracy rules: no claim of sold
listings, comps or market data; the one-scan-a-day limit wherever plans are
mentioned; no other company's name in Keywords. The full list of claims it does
not make is in the English file, under *Claims this listing does not make*.

## Read this before pasting

**A localization of its own.** In App Store Connect it is *English (U.K.)*,
added from the language menu on the version page. Without it, the UK store
falls back to the primary language's page, English (U.S.). That is Apple's
documented fallback; it was not checked against the live `gb` storefront.

**Version-scoped, like every locale.** Name, subtitle, keywords and
description go onto the next version you can still edit, never one in review.
The promotional text can be pasted any time once the localization exists.

**Values are in dollars.** The estimate is in USD whatever the phone's region,
so a UK user sees "$45–$90". The description says so, as the German and
Spanish pages do.

**Prices.** No figure appears. The UK price is Apple's own tier in pounds, not
a conversion of $4.99.

Point your camera at any preloved item — a jacket, a pair of trainers, a vintage camera, a designer bag — and our AI identifies it and estimates its resale value as a price range, with a confidence level.

Clothing, shoes, accessories, electronics, books, furniture, homeware, sports gear, toys and collectibles.

No more guessing at the till. No more leaving £100 flips on the shelf.

## Keywords (100 chars max)
thrift,whats,worth,second,hand,preloved,reselling,resell,flip,scanner,price,vintage,trainers

None of the name's or subtitle's words, and no other company's name: Vinted
dominates UK resale and is named in the description, where it is a feature,
but App Store Connect does not allow it as a keyword. `whats` and `worth`
together match "what's it worth"; `second` and `hand` match "second hand"
written as two words, which is how it is mostly typed here.
